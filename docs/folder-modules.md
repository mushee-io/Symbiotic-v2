# Folder — Longbow & Twofold on Symbiotic V2

User-approved name of top-level navigation section: **Folder**.
Inside Folder are exactly **Longbow** and **Twofold**. Each has its own
product-specific screen and independent development scope.

- `/folder` — Folder product chooser.
- `/folder/longbow` — planned stock-collateral borrowing.
- `/folder/twofold` — planned lending liquidity allocation.
- `/api/folder/products` — read-only product status for later integrations.

No debt, yield, loan or vault contracts are deployed or enabled by this
navigation milestone. All interactions currently navigate between product
pages; they do not authorize or simulate fund transfers.

Detailed terms remain subject to the user's instructions. Before enabling
Longbow: verified stock/token eligibility, price oracle, LTV and liquidation
thresholds, debt-interest accrual, interest and fee accounting, repayment,
collateral retrieval, stale-data protections and funded Preprod tests.
Before enabling Twofold: source and ownership of lent assets, deposit
shares, utilisation caps, reserve buffer, liquidity recall, withdrawal
priority, risk of loss, real yield accounting, emergency exit and
funded Preprod tests.

Use test-only assets until valid issuer and legal backing arrangements
exist for tokenised securities. Reference equity tickers do not give
stock ownership. Preserve existing five-validator deployment generation
unless a deliberate and audited protocol upgrade is conducted.
